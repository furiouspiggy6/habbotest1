Strategy & controls

Market connection

Live NFT market only. The app fetches orderbook listings and completed sales from Immutable. If the API fails, the market stays empty and displays the error. No simulated listings.

Buying signals

Spending protection

All dashboard values are shown in Australian dollars, using an approximate live ETH/AUD spot rate. Immutable transactions still settle in WETH. Waiting for a fresh quote.

Additional NFT marketplaces

OpenSea provides a separate live listing feed for verified Ethereum Habbo Avatars. Its API requires a key. Your key is saved locally and not displayed in Market Radar's status response. OpenSea NFTs are watch-only, not eligible for the Immutable autobuyer.

Get a free OpenSea key from the OpenSea developer portal. Other Immutable storefronts usually share our existing orderbook.

Data collection

Each page requests up to 50 REAL Immutable orders. New default: 20 pages per collection for active listings and completed sales. Increase up to 40 for deeper coverage; large scans may take longer or hit rate limits.
